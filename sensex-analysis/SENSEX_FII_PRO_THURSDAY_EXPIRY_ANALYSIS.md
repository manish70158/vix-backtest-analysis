## Why Separate Thursday Analysis?

The main analysis document blends 3 different expiry regimes:
- **Friday regime** (2020-2024): Strong weekly close effects, liquidation Friday afternoon
- **Tuesday regime** (Jan-Sep 2025): Mid-week behavior, transition period
- **Thursday regime** (Sep 4, 2025+): **CURRENT — Most relevant for forward trading**

**Key behavioral differences:**
- Thursday does NOT have Friday's "end-of-week" squeeze
- Thursday does NOT have Tuesday's "early-week" uncertainty
- Thursday patterns are unique: mid-week expiry → Monday positioning

## Thursday Expiry Behavioral Characteristics

### Intraday Pattern Tendencies

| Pattern | Frequency | Market Implication |
|---|---|---|
| **Down to Up** | ~45% (common) | Open down, bounce into Thursday close. Standard Thursday reversal. |
| **Top to Down** | ~40% (common) | Open up, trend down. Post-open profit taking into close. |
| **Neutral/Range** | ~15% (low) | Range-bound. Typically low volume Thursday close before Friday. |

**Why Thursday differs from Friday:**
- Friday has stronger end-of-week liquidation (more Down to Up reversals, bigger moves)
- Thursday sees position squaring for Monday, not weekend (different timing, less aggressive)
- Thursday close flows directly into Monday open (no weekend gap reset)

### FII/PRO Positioning Near Thursday Close

**Thursday vs Friday behavior:**
- **Friday**: Heavy FII/PRO adjustments 3:00-3:30 PM as they square for weekend
- **Thursday**: Lighter flow, more rollover positioning (taking Friday into account)
- **Thursday**: More prone to afternoon reversals if morning was wrong direction

**Key distinction**: Thursday morning moves are MORE likely to reverse if driven by one-sided flow, because Friday options still have 1 day value. Friday expiry has more finality in moves.

- Liquidation cascade potential

**Thursday afternoon** (2:00-3:30 PM):

- More positional adjustments (rolling, not closing)
- Reversals more common than continuation
