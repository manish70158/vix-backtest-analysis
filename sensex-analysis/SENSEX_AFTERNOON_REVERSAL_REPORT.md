## 11. Key Findings and Trading Implications

1. **The 14:00 time bucket is the most common inflection zone on Sensex expiry days**, with 45.0% of all afternoon reversals originating here. Traders should be positioned or alert by this time.

2. **Top 3 exact reversal times**: 14:00, 14:05, 14:20. These account for 47.5% of all inflections.

3. **Nifty proxy afternoon direction matches Sensex daily direction 65.4% of the time.** This confirms the strong intraday correlation and validates using Nifty as a proxy.

4. **Sensex-only expiry days show comparable or larger afternoon moves** (avg 0.362%) vs dual expiry days (avg 0.31%). Sensex-specific expiry dynamics are significant.

5. **High VIX days produce larger afternoon moves** (avg 0.55% from inflection) vs low VIX days (avg 0.3% from inflection).

6. **Top to Down is the dominant Sensex daily pattern on expiry days**, occurring on 54.1% of days.

7. **Expiry day schedule**: Friday (211), Thursday (46), Tuesday (35). Sensex shifted from Friday to Thursday/Tuesday expiry in 2025.
