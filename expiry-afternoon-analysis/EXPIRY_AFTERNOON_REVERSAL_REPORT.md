## 9. Concordance: Afternoon vs Full-Day Direction

The full-day `move_direction` from the CSV classifies each day based on open-to-close.
The afternoon classification uses only the 2 PM to 3:30 PM session.

- **Match**: 199 days (67.0%)
- **Mismatch**: 98 days (33.0%)
- **Flat (excluded)**: 12 days

A high concordance rate means the afternoon direction is consistent with the full-day trend.
A mismatch means the morning session moved opposite to the afternoon — the reversal happened before 2 PM.

## 10. Key Findings and Trading Implications

1. **The 14:00 time bucket is the most common inflection zone**, with 44.8% of all expiry day afternoon reversals originating here. Traders should be positioned or alert by this time.

2. **Top 3 exact reversal times**: 14:00, 14:15, 14:05. These three times alone account for 49.2% of all inflections.

3. **Afternoon direction matches full-day direction 67.0% of the time.** This means the post-2 PM move usually confirms the daily trend rather than reversing it.

4. **High VIX days produce larger afternoon moves** (avg 0.46% from inflection) vs low VIX days (avg 0.32% from inflection).

5. **Down to Up is the dominant afternoon pattern**, occurring on 48.9% of expiry days. This aligns with the general tendency of option premium decay pulling prices in the closing hours.
