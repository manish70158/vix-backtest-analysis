# FII Neutral — Behavior by PRO View (3-Year, 310 days)

## Key Findings

| PRO View | Days | Green% | Red% | Avg Change | Avg Range | Dominant Pattern |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Strong Bearish** | 33 | **60.6%** | 39.4% | +0.16% | 0.95% | Down to Up (60.6%) |
| **Bearish** | 66 | **57.6%** | 42.4% | +0.06% | 0.87% | Down to Up (57.6%) |
| **Neutral** | 60 | 48.3% | 51.7% | -0.04% | **1.02%** | Balanced |
| **Bullish** | 108 | **58.3%** | 41.7% | +0.06% | 0.89% | Down to Up (57.4%) |
| **Strong Bullish** | 44 | 43.2% | **56.8%** | -0.09% | 0.85% | Up to Down (56.8%) |

## Insights

### 1. Contrarian edge when PRO is bearish

When FII is Neutral and PRO is Strong Bearish, market closes Green 60.6% of the time with +0.16% avg change. The dominant move is "Down to Up" — market dips early then recovers.

### 2. Strong Bullish PRO actually fails

When PRO is extremely bullish but FII is just Neutral, market closes Red 56.8% of the time. The pattern is "Up to Down" — opens strong then fades. This is the worst combination.

### 3. Moderate Bullish PRO works

Plain Bullish PRO gives 58.3% Green with a similar "Down to Up" recovery pattern.

### 4. Both Neutral = highest volatility

When both FII and PRO are Neutral, the average range is the widest (1.02%) but direction is a coin flip (48.3% Green).

## Expiry vs Non-Expiry

The most striking difference is on **FII Neutral + PRO Strong Bearish**:

- **Non-expiry days**: 69.2% Green, +0.20% avg change
- **Expiry days**: Only 28.6% Green — completely reverses

This suggests on non-expiry days, the contrarian signal (bearish PRO, neutral FII) is strong. On expiry days, options-driven flows overwhelm it.

## Actionable Summary

- **Best setup**: FII Neutral + PRO Strong Bearish on non-expiry → buy the dip (69% win rate)
- **Avoid**: FII Neutral + PRO Strong Bullish → fades from highs (57% Red)
- **Expiry caution**: Patterns flip on expiry days, especially for bearish PRO views
