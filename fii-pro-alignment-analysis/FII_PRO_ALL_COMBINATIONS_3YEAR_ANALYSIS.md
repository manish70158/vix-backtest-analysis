## Conclusion

1. **Contrarian setups outperform**: Bearish FII + Bullish PRO is the strongest reversal signal
2. **Over-bullish fails**: Bullish FII + Strong Bullish PRO (59 days, 30.5% Green) is a consistent trap
3. **Aligned bullish works**: Both Strong Bullish = 64% Green (39 days, most reliable)
4. **Double bearish doesn't mean market falls**: Strong Bearish + Strong Bearish = only 48% Green
5. **"Down to Up" dominates winning combos** — morning weakness followed by afternoon strength
6. **Expiry days behave differently** — many setups flip direction on expiry
7. **Mildly Bearish PRO is toxic**: Across multiple FII views, Mildly Bearish PRO underperforms
