## 10. Key Findings and Trading Implications

1. **The 14:00 inflection zone dominates across ALL trading days**, with 49.9% of inflections starting in the 14:00 bucket. This is NOT unique to expiry days — it's a general market phenomenon.

2. **Expiry and non-expiry days share the same top inflection time** (14:00 for expiry, 14:00 for non-expiry). The afternoon move timing is driven by market microstructure, not options expiry.

3. **Concordance rate**: Expiry days 69.0% vs Non-expiry 67.7%. Virtually identical — expiry status doesn't change afternoon behavior.

4. **Afternoon move magnitudes are similar**: expiry 0.249% vs non-expiry 0.251%. Options expiry does not meaningfully amplify or dampen the afternoon move.

5. **When reversals happen**: On expiry days, 55.4% are morning sell-offs that recover after 2 PM vs 37% rallies that fade. On non-expiry days: 49.9% recoveries vs 170 fades. Expiry days favor afternoon recoveries more.
