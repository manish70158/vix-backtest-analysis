## Conclusion

### What the 6-year data confirms:

1. **"Mild" alignment is gold**: Mildly Bearish + Mildly Bearish (100%, 9d) and Mildly Bullish + Mildly Bullish (86%, 7d) are the top performers. Moderate views that agree produce the best outcomes.

2. **Strong Bullish PRO is a consistent trap when FII is bullish**: Bullish + Strong Bullish (115d, 36.5%) and Mildly Bullish + Strong Bullish (36d, 30.6%) are the two largest reliably bearish setups. Over-enthusiasm from PRO when FII is already bullish = market fades.

3. **Contrarian FII-PRO divergence works**: Bearish FII + Bullish PRO (83%), Strong Bearish FII + Bullish PRO (100%), Mildly Bearish FII + Strong Bullish PRO (86%) — when PRO opposes bearish FII, market recovers.

4. **Mildly Bearish PRO is toxic across most FII views**: Consistently the worst or near-worst PRO view within each FII category. Neutral Mildly Bearish = 38%, Bearish Mildly Bearish = 37.5%.

5. **Expiry days distort patterns**: Neutral + Mildly Bearish drops to 11% Green on expiry vs 45% on non-expiry. Strong Bearish + Strong Bearish = 20% on expiry vs 46% on non-expiry.

6. **"Down to Up" dominates all winning combos**: Every combination above 55% Green shows "Down to Up" as the dominant move — morning weakness followed by afternoon recovery.

### Actionable Rules:

- **Go long**: Mildly Bearish FII + Mildly Bearish PRO, or any Bearish FII + Bullish PRO
- **Go long (high confidence, large sample)**: Bullish FII + Neutral PRO (68%, 19d), Strong Bullish FII + Strong Bullish PRO (59%, 66d)
- **Avoid longs / consider shorts**: Bullish FII + Strong Bullish PRO (37%, 115d), Mildly Bullish FII + Strong Bullish PRO (31%, 36d)
- **Expiry caution**: Reduce position size on expiry days, patterns are less reliable
