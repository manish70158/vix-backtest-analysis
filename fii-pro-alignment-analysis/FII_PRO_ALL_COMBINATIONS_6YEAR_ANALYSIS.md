## Conclusion

### What the 6-year data confirms:

1. **"Mild" alignment is gold**: Mildly Bearish + Mildly Bearish (91%, 11d) and Mildly Bullish + Mildly Bullish are top performers. Moderate views that agree produce the best outcomes.

2. **Strong Bullish PRO is a consistent trap when FII is bullish**: Bullish + Strong Bullish (129d, 38.0%) and Mildly Bullish + Strong Bullish are the largest reliably bearish setups. Over-enthusiasm from PRO when FII is already bullish = market fades.

3. **Contrarian FII-PRO divergence works**: Bearish FII + Bullish PRO, Strong Bearish FII + Bullish PRO — when PRO opposes bearish FII, market recovers.

4. **Mildly Bearish PRO is toxic across most FII views**: Consistently the worst or near-worst PRO view within each FII category.

5. **Expiry days distort patterns**: Several setups flip direction on expiry — check divergence table.

6. **"Down to Up" dominates all winning combos**: Every combination above 55% Green shows "Down to Up" as the dominant move — morning weakness followed by afternoon recovery.

### Actionable Rules:

- **Go long**: Mildly Bearish+Mildly Bearish (91%, 11d), Bearish+Bullish (86%, 7d), Mildly Bearish+Strong Bullish (86%, 7d), Mildly Bullish+Mildly Bullish (80%, 10d), Strong Bullish+Bullish (80%, 5d), Bullish+Neutral (65%, 20d), Mildly Bullish+Neutral (61%, 28d)
- **Avoid longs / consider shorts**: Strong Bearish+Strong Bullish (20%, 5d), Mildly Bullish+Strong Bullish (32%, 40d), Strong Bearish+Neutral (33%, 9d), Strong Bullish+Neutral (33%, 6d), Bullish+Mildly Bearish (38%, 8d)
- **Expiry caution**: Reduce position size on expiry days, patterns are less reliable
